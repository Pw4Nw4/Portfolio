Who I am

I'm a founder, an AI strategist and a builder. I started F09 Tech because most businesses don't need more technology. They need the work itself to run better. So I start with people and process, and only then bring in the tools.

I've spent more than five years in AI and technology, from keeping critical systems, devices and networks running for busy operations teams to shaping AI strategy with business owners. That's why I care less about what's new and more about what holds up when the business is busy.

How I think

Business first. Technology second.

I don't lead with tools. I start with how your business runs today: your goals, your team, and the way work actually gets done. Only then do I recommend the strategy, automation, AI, infrastructure or security that fits.

  1. People
  2. Process
  3. Technology
  4. Automation + AI
  5. Better operations

  1. Discover

    I learn the workflows, priorities and constraints shaping your business today.

  2. Analyze

    I find the friction, risk and disconnected work holding the operation back.

  3. Improve

    I simplify the process before adding a platform, an automation or another layer.

  4. Automate

    I connect the repeatable work so information and action move with less manual effort.

  5. Secure

    I build protection into how you operate instead of attaching it afterward.

  6. Optimize

    I review what changed, strengthen what works and get the system ready to scale.
